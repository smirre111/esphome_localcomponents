Root cause: the LoRa DIO0/DIO1 ISRs (`main/myinterrupts.cpp`) are installed IRAM-safe but call `gpio_intr_disable()`, which lived in **flash** because `CONFIG_GPIO_CTRL_FUNC_IN_IRAM` was unset. If that ISR fires while the flash cache is disabled (e.g. a concurrent NVS write/erase on the other core), it faults fetching the instruction — and the panic handler it then needs was *also* flash-resident by default, so even the fault handler can't be reached. Both cores stall until the interrupt watchdog hard-resets with no backtrace. Sustained Mode B + MAC-1 traffic made this likely enough to hit in ~12-15 minutes because `SessionManager` persists to NVS every 16 accepted downlinks under the MAC-1 counter (N-3, 2026-10-01) — about one flash write every ~24s at that traffic's cadence. **Fix:** `CONFIG_GPIO_CTRL_FUNC_IN_IRAM=y` + `CONFIG_ESP_PANIC_HANDLER_IRAM=y` in `sdkconfig`.
